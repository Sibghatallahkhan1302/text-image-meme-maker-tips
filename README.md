# Text Image and Meme Maker Tips

Simple text images are easy to share and hard to get wrong. When you need a quick styled graphic, a [brat generator](https://bratgeneratortool.com/) can turn a short phrase into a ready-to-post image without any design skills. The tips below help any text-based graphic look sharper.

## Keep the message short

One idea per image works best. Short phrases are easier to read on a phone screen and more likely to be shared.

## Choose readable contrast

Dark text on a light background, or the reverse, keeps words clear. Avoid busy backgrounds that fight with the text.

## Pick one font style

A single plain font looks cleaner than a mix of styles. Stick to simple, widely available fonts.

## Check the size before posting

Square images suit profile pictures and playlist covers, while tall images suit stories. Preview your graphic at small size to be sure it stays readable.
